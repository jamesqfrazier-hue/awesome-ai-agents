Description

    Built Right, Down to the Studs

    Every detail meets NYS OCFS & ECERS‑3 standards.

    Nothing here is an afterthought — the room dividers, the shelf heights, the sinks — all of it was drafted before a single wall went up.

    Compliance Highlights

    • All materials sit at child height, in every room
    • Learning centers labeled clearly, with photos
    • Wide-open floor space for safe movement & supervision
    • Sinks & handwashing stations within reach
    • Natural daylight reaches every classroom
    • Exits marked, unobstructed, and drilled monthly

    Mobile & Modular by Design

    • Wall dividers roll and lock into place in seconds
    • Chairs stack, tables fold, storage rolls away
    • Rugs roll up in one motion for fast storage
    • Every classroom breaks down in under 20 minutes
    • Full open floor for Sunday service, restored by Monday
    • Locking casters keep every unit put during the week

    One Room, Two Callings

    Weekdays, it's a classroom.
    Weekends, it's a sanctuary.

    Tots 2 Teens shares its basement with the church upstairs. Every shelf, divider, and rug was chosen so the same 1,750 sq ft can flip from five classrooms to open sanctuary seating — and back — without a single tool.

    ⏱ Full changeover, start to finish: under 20 minutes
    1. Unlock & fold dividers
    2. Stack chairs, fold tables
    3. Roll up rugs, store toys
    4. Clear floor — sanctuary ready
